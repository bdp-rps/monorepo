Text is the primary component for displaying text of any kind.
It provides a set of variants in order to fit for any use case.

Text is a direct export of [ambrose](https://github.com/robinweser/ambrose)'s Text component.

### Basic Usage

```jsx
<Box space={2}>
  <Text variant="title">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="subtitle">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="category">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="body">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text>A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="label">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="note">A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog</Text>
  <Text variant="note" variant="info">
    A Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog
  </Text>
</Box>
```
